The community & its spaces

We grow local communities, then give them what they need.

Each Library About space exists to support the people inside it. We provide two things — resources and contexts — and the community does the rest.

Resources

A physical space, internet, workstations, our own software — and a large allowance of AI tokens to build with.

Contexts

Event formats like hackathons — but built around the real problems and research the community actually cares about.

How a community runs

Clear roles. Open rules. A transparent economy.

Roles
A core team holds the space; contributors, builders and the wider crowd each have a place around them.
Access
Membership defines who can use the space, the workstations and the shared token pool — and on what terms.
Rules
Light, legible governance the community can read, question and amend together.
Economy
Where resources come from and where they go is visible to everyone. No hidden ledger.

PVF — the digital twin

Automated storytelling for the living community.

Visibility is essential — but no format today fuses deep research with entertaining, mass-friendly content without a dedicated team to translate it. Members shouldn't burn half their energy explaining what they do.

PVF turns every signal the platform accumulates into an ongoing story — narrative storytelling inside digitized real spaces, visualizing the live relationships of real people, AI agents, ideas and processes.

A living creature
Participants, ideas and processes form one Tamagotchi-like organism.
Caretaker viewers
Online audiences watch and support it — through donations and other forms.
Always on
The story runs itself, so the work can speak without a translation team.
